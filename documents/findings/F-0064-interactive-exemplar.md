# F-0064: The interactive exemplar: a pure-Dart engine that agrees with the server, a countdown on fake time, and the hint as a contract rule

- **Date:** 2026-11-27
- **Author:** crew: Engineer (T-0064)
- **Type:** Design record / Lesson / Rules for the IX bricks
- **Related:** T-0064, P1-23, ADR-0014 (proposed), ADR-0015, ADR-0012, ADR-0010, R6, R15, R17; F-0056, F-0046, F-0059, F-0060, F-0058

## Summary
`trivia_quiz` is the seventh and last exemplar (IX): a setup (category, difficulty, best scores), a timed 10-question round (four choices, a countdown ring, a streak meter, instant feedback) and the results (score, correct count, best, a donut of outcomes and a bar chart of points). The rules are a pure-Dart engine inside the feature; the server rescores every round and its score wins. **No codegen shim failed on a real-world case**: the 14 freezed, json, retrofit and injectable outputs equal build_runner's (`T-0064-AC7_matches_build_runner.md`). The IX bricks copy sections 1 to 6; section 7 lists the traps.

## 1. The engine (ADR-0014)
- **Where:** `lib/src/domain/engine/`: `scoring.dart` (the contract's arithmetic: `judge`, `pointsFor`, the constants) and `trivia_engine.dart` (the round's state machine: `asking`, `revealed`, `finished`; `answer(choiceId, elapsedMs:)`, `timeOut()`, `useHint()`, `next()`).
- **Pure:** no Flutter, no `dart:async`, no clock, no floating point. It imports only its model (`trivia.dart`, `comic_id.dart`) and `freezed_annotation`/`mc_media`. Enforced twice: the `_engine` `dart_library` (only pure-Dart deps, analysed by `--config=lint`), and `:architecture_test`, which walks the engine's import closure and bans `double`, `num`, `/` and rounding calls. Its negative controls show a Flutter import, `dart:ui`, `dart:async`, `package:clock`, an import of a page, an `export` of Flutter and each floating-point form being caught, and `~/`, comments and strings not.
- **Time comes in as numbers:** the bloc measures; the engine judges. A question's `elapsedMs` is exactly what the bloc's clock read when the answer event was handled.
- **Tested through the flow first:** `trivia_quiz_scoring.feature`. Its Examples are the 17 worked examples of `mc_fixtures_editorial` CONTRACT.md **row for row**, and the 5 hint examples; `:outline_matches_contract_test` parses both tables and fails on a changed, missing or reordered row (negative controls). Each row holds the POST, checks that the score shown is the row's (the engine's), releases it, checks that the score shown is still the row's (the server's), that the POST body carries every answer as the tokens say (choice, exact `elapsedMs`, `hint`), and that no mismatch was reported.
- **Plus plain unit tests where the flow is slow or blind** (allowed since ADR-0015, which relaxes R6): `:engine_test` checks the hint's choice of what to hide (deterministic, never the correct one, any wrong one, its position gives nothing away) and the engine's refusals (a hidden or unknown choice, a second answer, `next()` before a reveal). The architecture test lets only `test/contract/` and `test/unit/` import `src/`, and `test/unit/` only the engine and its model.
- **No feature goldens (ADR-0015).** The painters are checked on a recording canvas in each of the 4 themes; visual ACs are widget tests (strings, no overflow at 1x and 2x, reduce motion).

## 2. Time under fake time
- **The flow takes `clock: int Function()` (monotonic ms) and `nextSeed: int Function()`**, as the store takes its clock. It provides `TriviaTicker(clock)` and `RoundSeeds(nextSeed)` in its container (not root imports, R17). The app passes `chronicleClock` (a `Stopwatch`) and `chronicleSeed` (`Random().nextInt(1 << 31)`); feature tests pass `triviaClock` (`TestWidgetsFlutterBinding.instance.clock`) and a seed list (42 by default).
- **Two timers per question, both real `Timer`s** (fake under `testWidgets`): a frame tick every 100 ms that redraws the countdown, and a one-shot at the question's remaining time that ends it **exactly at the limit**. `tester.pump(d)` moves the clock and the timers together, so `limit - 1` is still open and `limit` has timed out (`trivia_quiz_timer.feature`, every difficulty).
- **The countdown runs only while someone can play.** Three pause reasons: the app is not `resumed` (`inactive`, `hidden`, `paused` all pause: the app switcher, a call, the home screen), the flow's route is covered (`FlowScope.outerIsCurrent` false: another screen, a popup), and the quit dialog. The clock resumes when no reason is left; paused time never counts. Pinned: the background outline (3 states, 60 s away, then exactly 15 s left), "time in the background does not count towards an answer" (`Correct! +137` after 4 s + 90 s away + 1 s), `flow_scope_test` "a round covered by another screen", and the quit scenario (60 s under the dialog, then `Correct! +125`).
- **The quit dialog pauses first, then opens.** Quit (the close button, or a pop of the page) adds `quitAsked`; the bloc pauses and sets `confirmingQuit`, and the page's `BlocListener` opens `showMcConfirm`. Cancel resumes, Confirm leaves: to the setup, or out of the flow when it was opened at a round (`controller.complete()`, and the flow's `onComplete` pops its route). With no round yet (loading, failed) quitting leaves at once.
- **Reduce motion** (`MediaQuery.disableAnimations`): the ring is not drawn, the number only.

## 3. The results, the server and the session
- **The server's score is authoritative.** The results show the engine's score while the POST is on its way, then the server's. If they differ, the server's is shown and `TriviaScoreMismatch` is reported once to `mc_monitoring` (`the server scores every round {999}`: a test domain whose scenario overrides the route).
- **Signed in when the results open:** posted at once, pinned to that epoch (`authPinned`, T-0050). **Signed out:** the local score and "Save your score"; a tap awaits `requestSignIn()`; on true *with someone signed in* the result is posted for that user, pinned to the epoch read right after the answer; on false nothing is sent (also when someone signed in elsewhere meanwhile: the answer decides whether to save, the session whom for).
- **Both orders of a user switch:** a switch while the held POST is on its way drops its answer (the page offers Retry for the new user, who gets it on Retry); a switch just before the POST leaves is refused by the pin before any bearer is attached (0 POSTs reach the backend), and Retry saves for the new user.
- **A failed POST** keeps the local score with "Not saved" and Retry; Retry posts once (a second tap finds it saving).
- **The setup's best scores follow the session** (the cart pattern of F-0056 §2): read pinned to the epoch, dropped and read again when the epoch moved (also a same-user re-sign-in, which has no `authState` event), read again on every `authState` change and each time the page is shown again (a round above it may have set a new best). A failed read shows no best; the categories still work.
- **`openComic`** is on the results' question list (a question about one comic names it, see §4), guarded against a double tap (500 ms, the other exemplars' value).

## 4. The hint, and what the contract had to gain (in `mc_fixtures_editorial`)
- **Design: the hint is a contract rule, not a client trick.** An answer may carry `"hint": true`; a correct hinted answer is worth 50 points less (`100 + timeBonus + streakBonus - 50`, so at least 50); a hinted miss is 0; the streak is unchanged by it; at most one hinted answer per round (`too_many_hints`). The server applies the same rule, so the local and the server's scores still agree. CONTRACT.md has 5 hint examples (`H1` to `H5`, their own table, so the 17 scoring rows are unchanged), with snapshots `result_hint` and `too_many_hints`, validation tests, and the independent oracle counting the penalty down point by point.
- **The rejected alternative:** hiding two choices without changing the score (the brief's first example) would have made the hint free, and a client-only penalty would have made every hinted round a mismatch.
- **Which choices hide:** the wrong choice that stays is `wrong[fnv1a32("<roundId>/<questionId>") % 3]` (the wrong choices in shown order). Deterministic, and it does not give the answer away by position.
- **The flag:** the `trivia_hint` experiment (`control` 10000, `hint` 0 in `mc_fixtures_platform`, so only a dev override turns it on). The play bloc reads the variant once per round: a fetch that lands mid-round does not make the hint appear (`flow_scope_test`, mutant P05).
- **`comicId` on the wire:** questions of the seven one-comic templates now carry the comic's id (`comic_cast`, `comic_price`, `comic_year`, `comic_pages`, `creator_*`), so the results can open the comic. The provider test checks it is exactly those templates, equals the fact, and resolves on `GET /v1/comics/:id`.

## 5. Testing an interactive feature (rules the IX bricks copy)
1. **Never settle while a question is open.** The countdown schedules a frame every tick, so `mcSettle` or `pumpAndSettle` would wait for the question to time out, and its time would count. Play steps pump explicit durations; between questions they pump zero time (`landAtOnce`: six `pump(Duration.zero)`). Settle only on the setup and results pages.
2. **Open a timed page without a transition.** A round opened at the flow's root (`I open the round ... of seed ...`, the `/trivia/play` shape) has no page transition, so its first question starts at a known instant. A round started from the setup slides in for about 500 ms: its first answer must come after that (the setup-path scenarios start with `C1000` or `W1000`).
3. **An answer at or past the limit goes through the engine.** The countdown's timer is due at the limit and fake timers of the same instant run in creation order, so a tap scheduled for the limit would always come second. The step pumps `limit - 1`, moves the quiz's clock ahead (`clockAhead`: what a device does when a busy event loop runs the timer late), and taps: the engine judges `C20000` by its elapsed time, a timeout, as the server does. That is how the outline kills the engine's boundary mutant (`>=` to `>`), and why `C20000` is sent as the contract writes it.
4. **A no-answer (`N<ms>`) is played as letting the time run out**: the app has no "skip". It scores the same (0, the streak resets) and is sent as `choiceId: null` at the limit.
5. **Read facts past the feature.** The fixture round comes from a fresh `MockBackend` of the trivia domain (it must not see the scenario's failures or holds); the question on screen is matched by its prompt; the server's view of a result is the last POST body scored again on a fresh backend.
6. **Lifecycle under test:** walk the states one legal step at a time (`resumed`, `inactive`, `hidden`, `paused`) and back in a tear-down. **The app draws no frame while hidden or paused**, so a check of the screen in the background reads the last frame drawn; check after returning to `resumed`.
7. **Painters are tested from the tree** (no `src/` import, R7): take `CustomPaint.painter` by key and paint it on a recording canvas (F-0059 fact 1). The ring's sweep is `2π × share left` from 12 o'clock and turns urgent at a quarter; the meter lights one pill per answer in a row, with gaps; the donut's arcs are in proportion to the outcomes.

## 6. Shape (what the brick has)
- 3 pages (setup, play, results), 3 blocs with freezed sealed states, 2 custom painters plus the DS donut and bars, the engine, 4 endpoints (categories, rounds, results, best), 2 navigator methods (`openComic`, `requestSignIn`), 53 strings in en, de and en_XA.
- **Flow state** `{linked, round, result}`: the setup unless linked, then the play page while a round is set, or the results once a result is set. **The results replace the play page**, so Back from the results never returns to a finished round; for a linked round the results are the flow's root and Back leaves to `/trivia`.
- **Routes:** `/trivia` (`trivia_setup`) and its child `/trivia/play?category=&difficulty=&seed=` (`trivia_play`), each name the first page of its flow (ADR-0012). A cold link opens `[home, setup, round]`. A link with no values plays `all`/`easy` with a new seed. `AppLocations.trivia`/`triviaPlay(...)` have a drift test with spaces, `/` and non-ASCII.
- **Analytics:** `trivia_round_started` (`category`, `difficulty`) when the first question shows; `trivia_answer` (`correct`, `elapsed_bucket` = the quarter of the limit `q1`..`q4`, or `timeout`) per question; `trivia_round_finished` (`score`, the engine's, so it does not wait for the network or a sign-in).

## 7. Traps
1. **`emit` of the wrong type compiles nowhere**: a sealed state's case must be wrapped (`TriviaPlayState.playing(view)`); the first build failed on five emits.
2. **A bloc handler must return its future.** A `switch` expression that mixes `void` and `Future` arms in a `=>` body drops the futures, and an `emit` after the handler "completed" throws. Use a `switch` statement in an `async` handler.
3. **`go_router` keeps a popped child's query on the parent's location**: after quitting a linked round, the setup's location reads `/trivia?category=...`. Compare `uri.path`.
4. **The home placeholder is a lazy `ListView`**: a new entry above "Open placeholder 7" pushed it off the built range and broke `app_test`. The Trivia entry is last.
5. **A flow page's `PopScope` does not guard the flow's own route** (review round, fixed). It registers with the page's route inside the flow's navigator, so the iOS back swipe and a system back on the *outer* route popped the whole flow past the quit dialog (proved: both new `mini_client:trivia_route_test` cases fail without the fix). The play page now registers a `PopEntry` with `FlowScope.outerRoute` while a round is on screen: its `canPop` is false (which also turns the route's back swipe off) and a pop asks to quit. A linked round's own exit pops with `Navigator.pop`, not `maybePop`, since that pop is asked for. Every flow page that guards its pop needs the same; a kit helper in `mc_feature_flow` would be the general fix.
6. **The quit dialog is also a cover**: `showMcConfirm` uses the root navigator, so `outerIsCurrent` turns false and the covered pause applies too. The explicit dialog pause is kept (it holds from the tap, a frame before the dialog's route exists); a mutant that removes it alone is equivalent in tests and is not in the list.
7. **An answer the engine judges a timeout is drawn as one** (review round): a late tap of the correct choice used to get the green check of a right answer. Only a judged choice is marked now (check or cross); after a timeout the right answer is shown in green with no mark. The late tap is still sent as tapped (`C20000`), which the server judges a timeout as well.
8. **No `add` after an await or in a listener without `isClosed`** (review round): the quit dialog's answer (the page can be replaced while it is open), the setup's session listener, and a round that loads after the player quit (dropped by a `_left` flag: the page is still sliding out, so the bloc is not closed yet). A hint asked for while paused is refused and stays available.
